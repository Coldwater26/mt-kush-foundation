Apply for grants from the Montana Kush Community Foundation

Complete this form to request $500, $1,000, or $1,500 for your Montana community project. Review our programs, provide your contact and project details, select a grant amount, and explain how you will use the funds.

Is your project a fit?

We support nonprofits, local organizers, veteran groups, educators, and community projects doing useful work in Montana. Before you apply, explore our programs to find the best fit for your project.

  • Sports, Recreation, and Community Events
  • Public Education and Consumer Freedom
  • Montana Media Project
  • Patient Access and Consumer Education
  • Community Partnerships and Events

Tell us how your project connects with our programs and who it will help. Applying does not guarantee an award.
