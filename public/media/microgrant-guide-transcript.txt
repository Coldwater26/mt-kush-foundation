How to apply for a Foundation microgrant

Have an idea that could help your Montana community? Here's how to apply for a Montana Kush Community Foundation microgrant. We'll walk through the form, from choosing a program to sending your request. Start at Montana Kush dot org slash grants dot H T M L. Read the program descriptions, then gather your contact details, project plan, timeline, and estimated expenses. Fields marked with an asterisk are required. The form welcomes nonprofits, local organizers, veteran groups, educators, and community projects doing useful work in Montana. Review the six programs shown here and choose the closest fit for your project. Explain the connection in your application. Applying does not guarantee an award. Enter your contact name and email address, then select your applicant type. A phone number is optional. Double-check your email so the Foundation can contact you about your request. If you're applying for a group, add the organization name and your role or title. Individuals may leave the organization field blank. A website or project link is optional. If you include a link, use the full web address. Give your project a clear name and select its primary program. Then identify the Montana communities or counties it will serve. You can name a city, county, tribal community, or describe a statewide project. Describe what you plan to do, why it is needed, and how it connects to the program you selected. Use plain language and concrete activities. For example, describe a neighborhood cleanup and the local need it addresses. This field allows up to two thousand characters. Explain who will benefit, roughly how many people you'll serve, and how you'll know the project helped. This response allows up to fifteen hundred characters. Add your project dates or estimated timeline, including when you need the funds. Choose one of three grant amounts: five hundred, one thousand, or fifteen hundred dollars. Describe your overall project budget. The form accepts an estimated amount, a range, or an explanation. You can also list other funding secured or pending. That field is optional. In the use of funds field, list the expenses the grant will cover and an estimated cost for each. For illustration, a five hundred dollar request could list two hundred for supplies, two hundred for equipment rental, and one hundred for disposal. These are example costs, not a promise of eligibility. Use your own estimates. Read and check all three acknowledgments: program review, accuracy and authority to apply, and permission to review your details and contact you. The form uses FormSubmit to deliver applications. Do not include Social Security numbers, banking details, or private medical information. Select Review application to check your answers. Use Edit application if you need to make a change. When you're ready, select Submit application, then complete the verification step on the next screen. Reviewing your answers alone does not send your request. Ready to take the next step? Visit the grant page or scan the code to begin. For application questions, email Pepper at Montana Kush dot org. Montana Kush Community Foundation. Supporting the communities that support us.
