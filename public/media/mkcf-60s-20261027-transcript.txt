Supporting the communities that support us
Montana Kush Community Foundation
Published October 27, 2026 • 60 seconds

When flooding hit Butte's Covellite Theatre, Montana Kush stepped up. A forty-eight-hundred-dollar gift. A relationship built through years of concerts and community events. Across Montana, that commitment supports food and winter-clothing drives, blood drives, adult athletics, and local arts. From Helena's Alive at Five to Bozeman's Music on Main. Up to three hundred fifty thousand dollars in estimated community support. Now, the Montana Kush Community Foundation is building the next chapter: local partnerships, veterans, education, and community service. And your idea could be next. Applications are open for projects statewide. Fifteen thousand dollars is available, in awards of five hundred, one thousand, or fifteen hundred dollars. Apply by December fifteenth, twenty twenty-six. Awards announced January twenty twenty-seven. The Montana Kush Community Foundation. Supporting the communities that support us.
