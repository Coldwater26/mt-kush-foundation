Montana Kush Community Foundation Opens Applications for $15,000 in Statewide Microgrants

Grants of $500, $1,000 and $1,500 available for Montana community projects; applications due December 15

HELENA, Mont. Community groups across Montana can apply through December 15, 2026, for grants of $500, $1,000 or $1,500 from the Montana Kush Community Foundation. The foundation is making $15,000 available this cycle for projects supporting veterans, sports and recreation, public education and community events.

Awards will be announced in January 2027 as part of the foundation’s quarterly giving program. Applications are available at www.montanakush.org/grants.html.

“A small grant can put equipment on a field or supplies in volunteers’ hands. Tell us what your community needs and how you’ll make it happen,” said Bryce Hulet, the foundation’s board chair.

The program builds on Montana Kush’s community giving, including $4,800 in emergency support for the Covellite in Butte following flooding.

“When the Covellite flooded, Montana Kush stepped up with $4,800 in emergency support. The help they provided saved our upcoming productions in a time where our insurance refused to provide aid,” said Demetrius Fassas, director of Covellite Presents.

Applications are welcome from nonprofits, community groups, local organizers, educators and individuals proposing community projects. The program serves Montana statewide, including rural communities and towns. Applicants should describe the project, who will benefit, the amount requested and how the funds will be used.

The Montana Kush Community Foundation supports community projects across Montana. Its founding supporter, Montana Kush, operates dispensaries in Bozeman, Butte, Gallatin Gateway, Helena, Missoula and Whitefish.

Apply at www.montanakush.org/grants.html by December 15, 2026.

Photos and video related to the Covellite flooding are available to media upon request.
